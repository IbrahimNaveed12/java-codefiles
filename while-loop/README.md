# While Loop Practice

## Problem Title & Summary
Print all even numbers from 1 to 10 using a `while` loop.

## Logic & Approach
The program initializes a counter at 1 and keeps looping until it reaches 10. If the current number is even, it prints it; otherwise it skips it.

## Time & Space Complexity
Time Complexity: O(n)
Space Complexity: O(1)

## Example Input & Output
```text
2 is even
4 is even
6 is even
8 is even
10 is even
```
