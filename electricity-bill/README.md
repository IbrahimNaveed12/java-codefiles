# Electricity Bill

## Problem Title & Summary
Calculate a customer’s electricity bill from the units consumed and the price per unit.

## Logic & Approach
The program reads the customer name, unit count, and price per unit, multiplies them to find the total charge, and prints the result.

## Time & Space Complexity
Time Complexity: O(1)
Space Complexity: O(1)

## Example Input & Output
```text
---- Electricity Bill ----
Customer Name: Ali
Units Consumed: 100
Price per Unit: 12

Total Bill: 1200.0
```
