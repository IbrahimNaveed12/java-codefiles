# Travel Cost

## Problem Title & Summary
Estimate the total fuel required and the estimated trip cost based on distance, fuel consumption, and fuel price.

## Logic & Approach
The program reads the traveler’s name, distance, fuel economy, and fuel price. It then divides the distance by the fuel consumption to get the fuel needed and multiplies that amount by the fuel price to calculate the total cost.

## Time & Space Complexity
Time Complexity: O(1)
Space Complexity: O(1)

## Example Input & Output
```text
--- Travel Cost ---
Traveler Name : Ali
Distance: 300
Fuel Consumption: 20
Fuel Price: 120

Fuel Required: 15.0per liter
Estimated Fuel Cost: 1800.0
```
