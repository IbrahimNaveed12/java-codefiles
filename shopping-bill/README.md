# Shopping Bill

## Problem Title & Summary
Calculate the total cost of three products by multiplying price and quantity, then print the final bill.

## Logic & Approach
The program asks for the price and quantity of each product, calculates the subtotal for each item, and adds all subtotals to compute the final bill.

## Time & Space Complexity
Time Complexity: O(1)
Space Complexity: O(1)

## Example Input & Output
```text
---- Shopping Bill ----
Product 1: Notebook
Price: 50
Quantity: 2
Product 2: Pen
Price: 10
Quantity: 3
Product 3: Eraser
Price: 5
Quantity: 4

Final Bill: 180.0
```
